About Us

Shoemaker Painting has served Central Illinois for over 50 years, beginning in El Paso in 1975 and proudly calling Normal, Illinois, home for the past three decades.

As a family-owned company, we've built our reputation on a simple commitment: putting our customers first. From protecting your furniture and flooring to performing daily site cleanups, we treat your property with the utmost care and deliver professional results with minimal disruption to your daily routine.

Our Founder, Dennis Shoemaker
